5 industrial water treatment plants delivered in Qatar

Between 2016 and 2021, we delivered 5 industrial water treatment plants across Qatar under full EPC contracts – including engineering, procurement, construction, commissioning, and O&M through to handover. These projects demonstrate our execution capability, quality systems, and HSE standards – which we now bring to solar PV and civil infrastructure projects.
