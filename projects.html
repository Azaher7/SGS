Utility-Scale Solar PV + BESS · Kajiado County, Kenya

Kimuka 2 Solar PV + BESS Project — 50.64 MWp DC / 40 MWac

A 50.64 MWp DC / 40 MWac single-axis-tracker solar farm with a 10 MW / 20.06 MWh battery energy storage system, connected at 66 kV under a 20-year power purchase agreement. SGS has been selected as EPC contractor; the project is in development, with the EPC contract pending execution.

Industrial Wastewater EPC + 24/7 O&M · Qatar · 2016–2021

Qatar Industrial Wastewater Treatment Programme — five C-WWTP plants

Engineering, construction, commissioning and continuous 24/7 operation of five industrial wastewater treatment plants across three sites, delivered to the discharge standards of Qatar's Public Works Authority over roughly five years of regulated operations.
