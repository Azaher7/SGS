Qatar Industrial Wastewater Treatment Programme

Engineering, construction, commissioning and continuous 24/7 operation of five industrial wastewater treatment plants across three sites — delivered to the discharge standards of Qatar's Public Works Authority.

Programme Overview

Five regulated plants, one continuous operation

Between 2016 and 2021, SGS acted as the on-the-ground EPC and operations contractor for five industrial wastewater treatment plants (C-WWTP) in Qatar, delivered to the discharge standards of Qatar's Public Works Authority — one of the Gulf's most demanding infrastructure oversight frameworks.

Each plant treated contaminated groundwater generated by construction drilling and drainage. After treatment, effluent was re-injected into deep bore wells in compliance with the authority's discharge limits. SGS handled civil works, plant installation, commissioning and continuous operations.

Across roughly five years of regulated, round-the-clock operations, SGS built a documented track record of multi-site delivery — at one stage operating all five plants concurrently with a single field operations team.

Delivery

Scope delivered

  • Civil and structural works — reinforced-concrete bases, steel shelters, lined lagoons and tank fabrication
  • Plant installation and electromechanical works — pumps, filtration, dosing and electrical systems
  • Commissioning to regulatory acceptance
  • Continuous 24/7 operations and maintenance
  • Regulatory compliance and daily quality reporting
  • Chemical, sludge, fuel and consumables management
  • Concurrent multi-plant, multi-site programme management

Treatment process

  • Influent — contaminated construction groundwater
  • Removal of suspended solids, oils and grease
  • Removal of heavy metals such as manganese and iron
  • Reduction of organic and biological load
  • Filtration and disinfection stages
  • Effluent re-injection into deep bore wells
  • Compliance with Public Works Authority discharge limits
