Project Overview

Utility-scale solar and storage in Kenya's Rift Valley

SGS International has been selected as EPC contractor for the Kimuka 2 solar project, a utility-scale photovoltaic and battery storage facility under development by a Kenyan independent power producer in Kajiado County, Kenya.

Following a structured technical and commercial evaluation, SGS received a formal notification of intention to award for the full EPC scope and accepted it. The project is currently in development: final contract negotiations are underway and the EPC contract is pending execution. Kimuka 2 forms part of SGS's active project portfolio — it is an awarded, in-development project rather than a completed reference.

Once contracts are executed, the plant is planned for delivery on a 12-month EPC programme, targeting commercial operation in 2028 under a 20-year power purchase agreement with Kenya Power and Lighting Company (KPLC).

EPC scope of work

  • Detailed engineering and design — IFC drawings, DC/AC electrical studies, protection coordination
  • Procurement and supply of all plant, equipment and materials
  • Civil and structural works — tracker foundations, access roads, drainage
  • Electromechanical installation — modules, trackers, inverters, MV stations
  • Battery energy storage integration — BMS / EMS commissioning
  • HV substation and 66 kV overhead transmission line
  • Grid interconnection and grid-code compliance
  • Testing, commissioning and performance-ratio testing
  • Handover documentation and operations readiness
  • Operations & maintenance through the defects liability period
