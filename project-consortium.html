Consortium Leadership

SGS International — main EPC contractor & leader

SGS International operates as the single-counterparty EPC contractor and consortium leader: the client interface from feasibility and detailed engineering through procurement, construction, commissioning, and long-term operations.

Around that core, the consortium adds European environmental technology, dedicated solar engineering, and structured finance — so a project can be engineered, financed, built, connected and operated under one coordinated structure.

SGS responsibilities

  • Client interface and single-counterparty EPC delivery
  • Engineering design and design approval
  • Project and construction management
  • Procurement strategy and direct OEM relationships
  • Commissioning and grid connection
  • Long-term operations and maintenance
  • Stakeholder and authority coordination

Execution Model

One structure, full project lifecycle

SGS leads every phase as EPC contractor, with consortium partners supporting where their specialism adds the most value.

Legal & contracts

SGS leads contracting, permitting and compliance interfaces, with consortium finance and local-counsel support.
